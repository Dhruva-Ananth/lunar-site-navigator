Step 1 Resources

Say which mineral / volatile deposits matter. Weight ranks cells; min / max are hard limits that remove area. The globe shows what still passes, coloured by score.

More elements

Score = weighted mean of each layer normalised to 0–1 over its 2–98 percentile range (flipped where “low” is preferred). “Best N%” buttons set the limit at an area-weighted percentile of the real map.

Step 2 Terrain

Elevation
Slope

Slope here is a regional slope computed from the global LOLA grid (~7.6 km per pixel, ~15 km baseline, averaged per grid cell). It is coarse: lander-scale slopes (metres) are steeper and are not resolved. Use “Analyse area in detail” in step 4 for the finer grid.

Step 3 Communications

Link availability

Choose Direct-to-Earth (DSN) or a lunar relay in the menu on the right; the availability map and the suitable area update with it. Click the Moon to pin a point and see approximate link times there. Availability is a geometric model (no local terrain horizon), not a measurement.

Step 4 Select site

Matching areas

These areas come from coarse global maps: Lunar Prospector element maps have ~15–150 km pixels and the LOLA grid used here is 7.6 km per pixel, evaluated on a 1° grid. Treat the outlines and km² as regional guidance, not surveyed boundaries.
